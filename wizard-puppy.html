Privacy Policy

Wizard Puppy: Magic Sort • Last updated: October 15, 2026

Wizard Puppy: Magic Sort ("the App", "we", "our") is an enchanted puzzle game developed for players of all ages. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how data is handled.

1. Data Processed Locally

All core puzzle gameplay, level generation, sound synthesis, and game state mechanics execute 100% locally on your device:

  • Game Progress & Settings: Saved locally on your device via Android's encrypted SharedPreferences (highest level unlocked, stars earned per level, sound volume, and haptic vibration preferences).
  • This gameplay data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote developer server.
  • You can clear this data at any time by wiping app storage in your Android device Settings.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads & UMP)

To support development while keeping the game free and fully playable, the App integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK:

  • Anchored Adaptive Banners: Displayed on non-gameplay screens (Home and Level Selection) without interrupting puzzles. Never shown during active puzzle gameplay.
  • Natural Victory Interstitials: Displayed only after every 5 completed levels (5, 10, 15, 20...) at natural victory transitions. If a rewarded ad was recently viewed during that level, the interstitial is automatically skipped.
  • Rewarded Divination Hints: Completely optional. If players choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive a hint on the optimal next move, an ad is requested. If the device is offline or an ad cannot load, the hint remains freely available without forcing payment or waiting.
  • User Messaging Platform (Consent): Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and applicable jurisdictions are presented with a consent dialog to manage their advertising preferences in compliance with GDPR, ePrivacy, and CCPA/CPRA.
  • Privacy Choices Button: Players can review or adjust their ad privacy preferences at any time in the in-game Settings dialog by tapping "Ad & Privacy Choices".

Information Processed by Google AdMob:

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) for ad serving and frequency capping (subject to user consent).
  • Diagnostic and Performance Data: App crash logs, ad load latency, and interaction telemetry.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic location derived from IP address for regional ad delivery.

For more details, visit Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Children's Privacy

Wizard Puppy: Magic Sort does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Ad requests use standard age-appropriate flags configured in the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
